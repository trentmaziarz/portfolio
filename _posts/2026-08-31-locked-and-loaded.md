Colton went out of their way to work with the Knights Hall community to try and source some loaner armor for us (I wore George's, the enthusiastic man above me in the group photo). To get us not only a real fight and real experience in armor early in our training, but also connect us with others in the community and possibly participate in Rookie Rumble in it (planned, *fingers crossed*).
